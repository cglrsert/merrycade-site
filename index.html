How it works

From your memories to their adventure in four steps

No design skills, no account and no waiting. Everything happens on your phone, at your own pace — and it saves as you go.

Add the two heroes

Pick a photo of each of you. Merrycade turns your faces into storybook heroes right on your device.

Write your story

Choose what your characters say in 16 story moments — from the first meeting to the final goodbye.

Fill it with memories

Hide photos and notes in ten gift boxes, record voice messages and pick the music for each moment.

Send the invitation

Share it with Messages, WhatsApp, AirDrop or e-mail. They install Merrycade for free and play it.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does the person I send it to need to pay?

No. Merrycade is free to download, and opening and playing a gift is always free. Only creating gifts needs the one-time Creator purchase.

How do I send the gift?

When your adventure is ready, tap Invite. Merrycade creates a small .ccgift file that you send with any app you like — Messages, WhatsApp, AirDrop, e-mail or Files.

Can they play it on another platform?

Yes. Invitations work across iPhone, iPad and Android — they just need to install Merrycade.

Do you upload my photos?

No. There are no Merrycade servers. Your photos, words and voice stay on your device and travel only inside the invitation file you choose to send.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and Android phones and tablets with Android 7.0 or later.

Which languages?

English and Turkish. You can write your story in any language you like.
