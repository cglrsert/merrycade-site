How do I create a gift?

Tap Create a Gift Adventure, add both heroes' names and face photos, dress them, write what they say in the story moments, fill the gift boxes with photos and notes, then write your letter and wrap the final gift. Creating gifts is a one-time purchase; you can make as many as you like.

How do I send it?

When everything is ticked in the review, tap Invite. Merrycade creates a small .ccgift file that you send with WhatsApp, Messages, AirDrop, e-mail or Files.

How do they open it?

  • iPhone / iPad: install Merrycade (free), tap the file, tap Share and choose Merrycade. Or open the app → Open an Invitation and pick the file.
  • Android: install Merrycade (free) from Google Play, tap the file and choose “Open with Merrycade”. If your messaging app only saves it, use Open an Invitation in the app.

Receiving and playing a gift is always free.

I bought Creator access on another phone

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or in Google Play's order history.

Nasıl gönderilir?

Son kontrolde her şey işaretlenince Davet et'e dokun. Merrycade küçük bir .ccgift dosyası oluşturur; onu WhatsApp, Mesajlar, AirDrop, e-posta ya da Dosyalar ile gönder.

Karşı taraf nasıl açar?

  • iPhone / iPad: Merrycade'i (ücretsiz) yükle, dosyaya dokun, Paylaş'a dokunup Merrycade'i seç. Ya da uygulamayı aç → Bir Davet Aç ve dosyayı seç.
  • Android: Merrycade'i Google Play'den (ücretsiz) yükle, dosyaya dokun ve “Merrycade ile aç”ı seç. Mesajlaşma uygulaman yalnızca kaydediyorsa uygulamada Bir Davet Aç'ı kullan.
